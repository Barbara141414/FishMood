About the project关于这个项目

Learning to care for my fish friends better想更好地关心我的鱼儿朋友

I started by filming the tank's daily life, then built an interactive 3D tank, and used logistic regression and a hidden Markov model (HMM) to try to read the fish's states.我先用视频记下鱼缸的日常，再做了一个可以操作的三维鱼缸，并用逻辑回归和隐马尔可夫模型（HMM）试着读出鱼的状态。

How to tell them apart怎么认出它们

Three fish, three patterns三条鱼，三种花色

Shadow has blue-silver stripes, Cherry a splash of red on the head, and Bubbles black-and-white patches. In the footage you can tell them apart by their colours.阴影是蓝银条纹，樱桃头顶一抹红，泡泡身上是黑白花斑。在录像里看它们，按花色就能分辨。
